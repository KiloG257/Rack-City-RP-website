Rules

The code of the city

  1. Stay in character. No meta-gaming, no powergaming, no breaking immersion.
  2. Value your life. Act like you want to live — Fear RP always applies.
  3. No RDM / VDM. Every conflict needs a roleplay reason and initiation.
  4. Respect everyone. No harassment, hate or toxicity in or out of the city.
  5. No exploits or cheats. Bugs get reported, not abused. Cheating is a permanent ban.
  6. Staff have the final say. Disagree? Take it to a ticket, not the street.

Full rulebook is shared in Discord after whitelist approval.
